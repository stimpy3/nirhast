Build a mobile React web prototype of Nirhast, a hands-free kitchen assistant for home food businesses taking WhatsApp orders.

YOUR USERS AND THEIR LANGUAGES
A cook-owner in a noisy kitchen; a helper and delivery person who also speak; customers texting on WhatsApp. Mixed Hindi, Marathi and English. Expect Marathi-accented Hindi, slow older speakers and rushed, clipped replies. Editable synonyms: kanda = pyaaz, poli = roti.

THE VOICE FLOW, STEP BY STEP
The mic stays open; no button or wake word. After a question, wait up to 6 seconds for her to start. One question at a time.
1. Menu. Owner: "Aaj dal, roti. Thali 120." Nirhast: "Dal, roti, thali 120. Sahi?" Shows: menu card.
2. Customer texts. Answer greetings, menu, price and timing from today's menu. If quantity, time or address is missing, or a message is unclear, ask one question; never guess. Offer regulars their usual order. Owner-only questions go to the owner queue; only complete orders reach the kitchen.
3. New order. Nirhast: "Sharma ji, 2 thali, 1 baje. Haan?" Shows: order card. Cook: yes (customer gets "Order confirmed, 1 baje tak" once saved), no (polite decline), "baad mein" (re-ask in 5 minutes), or "2 nahi, 3" (updated card).
4. Owner queue, when idle. Nirhast: "Gupta ji pooch rahe hain: paneer hai aaj?" Cook: "Nahi, kal milega." Nirhast: "Bhej doon?" Yes sends the shown draft.
5. Next dish. Cook: "Ab kya banaun?" Nirhast: "Pehle Sharma ji, 1 baje. 6 thali ek saath." Shows: list ranked by earliest promised time, then smallest order; group same dishes.
6. Quiet mode. Cook: "Abhi mat bolo." Nirhast: "10 minute?" Cook: yes or a number. Shows: countdown. Then: "3 naye order. Pehle Sharma ji?" Only late alerts interrupt: "Gupta ji ka order 10 minute mein due."
7. Closing, 30 minutes before: "2 order baaki. Naye order band karoon?" Yes pauses new orders.
8. Cook: "Aaj kitne thali?" Nirhast: "28 thali, 4 bina pyaaz." Delivery person: "Sharma delivered." Nirhast: "Noted." Shows: delivered.
9. Cancel, refund, price change: owner repeats a random on-screen phrase. To a helper: "Yeh sirf owner kar sakti hain."

WHAT THE PRODUCT SAYS OR SHOWS BACK
Spoken lines: under 10 words, in the cook's language, with "ji" or "aap". A soft chime ends each question. Screen states: "Sun rahi hoon", words heard, "Saved". Each spoken line shows as a large card. Numbers: spoken as words, shown as digits ("teen, 3"). Delays over 1 second: "Ek second". Questions need a clear yes or no; updates get "Noted".

NOISE, ACCENTS, MIXED LANGUAGES AND MISHEARD INPUT
AI labels each reply yes, no or unclear. Examples: yes (haan, haan haan, ho, theek hai, chalega), no (nahi, nako). Only a clear yes accepts; noise never counts as yes.
Error ladder (unclear reply): 1st "Phir boliye." 2nd "Haan ya nahi boliye." 3rd: leave it pending with large Haan/Nahi buttons.
Nothing heard: repeat the question once, then the ladder.
Read back quantities and times. Treat पांच, 5 and ५ as equal. Quantity over 3 times the customer's usual: "Das thali? Sach mein?"
Replies end after 1 second of silence; 2 when expecting a number or correction.
If the cook speaks, stop talking; "ruko" silences Nirhast. Ignore Nirhast's own voice.
"Galat, wapas" within 15 seconds undoes the last action.
If speech recognition or saving fails, say "Abhi sun nahi pa rahi", show buttons. Never confirm to a customer before saving; never fail silently.

BUILD DETAILS
Screens: simulated WhatsApp chat, kitchen screen (large text, mic state, quiet badge), owner screen (menu, orders, customers, question queue).
Data (browser storage, this kitchen only): menu (dish, price), opening hours, customers (phone, name, address, usual order), orders (items, quantity, promised time, status: new, confirmed, delivered, cancelled), question queue, action log (time, action, speaker).
Wispr Flow API for speech-to-text, hinting menu and customer names if supported; text-to-speech out. The AI only classifies, extracts and phrases replies from owner data; ranking, timers, retries and owner checks are fixed rules.
Queue simultaneous orders; flag duplicates and ask; read post-confirmation edits as changes.
Label DEMO: WhatsApp chat (one replaceable module), sync, optional voice profiles ("Speaking as" switch), the phrase check. Offline: "saved on phone, will sync".
Never swap in other services silently; label substitutes DEMO and list them.
Sample data: 6 dishes, 8 customers, 3 regulars; paneer off today's menu. Out of scope: payments, delivery routing, customer logins.

DONE WHEN
"Hi" gets the menu; "paneer hai aaj?" reaches the owner queue; no address triggers a question; "haan" confirms the order in chat; quiet mode holds all but late alerts; a helper cannot cancel. Run every check, show a pass/fail table, then score your build on voice flow, failure handling and prompt match. If any check fails or any score is under 75%, fix and retest, up to 3 rounds; then list what still fails. Never fake a pass.
